# Resona DevDay demo script

For Keith to narrate. Target: under 90 seconds, including the short captures.

## Title and hook

> This is Resona, a phone-based wellness check-in. We built it for the Watcha hackathon, and today we have brought it back with Codex.

## Problem

> Most of us already have a phone in our pocket. What if we could use it for a quick check-in, without needing a wearable?

## Solution

> Resona brings breath, stillness and walking into one place, with an optional camera-based pulse estimate. These readings are experimental, not a medical assessment.

## Live demo

> Let me show you. Reports are on by default, and I can switch them off before recording. I record a breath on my phone.

**On screen:** Open the app, point out the report setting, record a breath and show the automatic report. Move to motion, show the stillness report, then cut to the walking capture and its cadence reading. Shorten capture footage as needed to stay within the time limit.

> With reports on, derived numbers go to OpenAI and a report appears after each capture, including stillness. A Codex model selects a short recording tip from fixed options. Raw audio, video and motion stay on my phone.

## Close

> Codex also helped us improve and test the existing app. Resona: every body has a rhythm.
