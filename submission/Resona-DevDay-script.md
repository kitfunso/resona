# Resona DevDay demo script

For Keith to narrate. Target: under 90 seconds, including the short captures.

## Title and hook

> This is Resona, a phone-based wellness check-in. We built it for the Watcha hackathon, and today we have brought it back with Codex.

## Problem

> Most of us already have a phone in our pocket. What if we could use it for a quick check-in, without needing a wearable?

## Solution

> Resona brings breath, stillness and walking into one place, with an optional camera-based pulse estimate. These readings are experimental, not a medical assessment.

## Live demo

> Let me show you. Before I start, I choose to share my derived numbers for reports. Then I record a breath on my phone.

**On screen:** Open the app, opt in to reports, record a breath and show the automatic report. Follow the on-screen prompts to the motion and heart modules; cut to the walking capture and its cadence reading. Shorten capture footage as needed to stay within the time limit.

> Once I opt in, the derived numbers go to OpenAI and a report appears after each completed module. A Codex model selects a short recording tip from fixed options. Raw audio, video and motion stay on my phone.

## Close

> Codex also helped us improve and test the existing app. Resona: every body has a rhythm.
