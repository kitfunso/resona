# Resona DevDay demo script

For Keith to narrate. Target: under 90 seconds, including the short captures.

## Title and hook

> This is Resona, a phone-based wellness check-in. We built it for the Watcha hackathon, and today we have brought it back with Codex.

## Problem

> Most of us already have a phone with sensors in our pocket. What if we could use it for a quick personal check-in, without needing a wearable?

## Solution

> Resona brings breath, stillness and walking into one place. You can also try an experimental camera-based pulse estimate. These are experimental readings, not a medical assessment.

## Live demo

> Let me show you. I open Resona on my phone, choose breath, and follow the instructions.

**On screen:** Open the app, record a breath, then show the reading. Cut to the walking capture and its cadence reading; use a short recording if needed to stay within the time limit.

> The raw sound and motion samples are processed on the phone. The result is a simple way to explore your own readings.

## Close

> Codex helped us improve the existing app and test its privacy and sensor flows. We are working on a Codex report for the readings; that part is currently unavailable. Resona: every body has a rhythm.
